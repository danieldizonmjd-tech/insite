Everything you need to know about InSite

InSite aggregates data from authoritative sources including the U.S. Census Bureau, Zillow ZHVI for property values, FBI crime statistics, Department of Education school ratings, and local government records. All data is verified and updated regularly to ensure accuracy.

Property value data is updated monthly from Zillow ZHVI. Demographic data is updated quarterly from the U.S. Census Bureau. Crime statistics are updated bi-annually from FBI reports. We display the last update date for each data point to ensure transparency.
