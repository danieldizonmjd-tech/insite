## Quick Test Steps:

### TEST 1: Explore Tab - Transit & Parks Scores
1. Click on the "Explore" tab (should be active by default)
2. In the search box, type: **30308** (Midtown Atlanta - high transit area)
3. Click "Search"
4. Scroll through the stats grid - you should see:
   - 🚇 Transit Score card (should be 90-100 for Midtown)
   - 🌳 Parks & Green Space card (should be 70-90 for Midtown)
5. Scroll down to see detailed sections:
   - "Public Transit Information" section with MARTA stations
   - "Parks & Recreation" section with nearby parks

### TEST 2: Lifestyle Tab - Best Transit Access
1. Click on the "Lifestyle" tab
2. Scroll down to the "🚇 Best Transit Access" section
3. Click the "Show Transit-Accessible Areas" button
4. You should see 8 neighborhoods displayed with:
   - Transit scores (90-100 for top areas)
   - Nearest MARTA station names
   - Distance in miles
5. Click on any result to explore it in the Explore tab

### TEST 3: Lifestyle Tab - Best Parks & Nature
1. Stay in the "Lifestyle" tab
2. Scroll down to the "🌳 Best Parks & Nature" section
3. Click the "Show Parks-Rich Areas" button
4. You should see 8 neighborhoods displayed with:
   - Parks scores (70-100 for top areas)
   - Number of parks within 1 mile
   - Total acres of green space
5. Click on any result to explore it in the Explore tab

### TEST 4: Lifestyle Quiz - Walkable Priority
1. In the Lifestyle tab, scroll to the top
2. Check the "Walkable" checkbox
3. Click "Find Neighborhoods That Match"
4. Results should show:
   - High transit scores for walkable areas
   - "Excellent transit access" or "Good transit options" in reasons
5. Each result card shows transit score

### TEST 5: Lifestyle Quiz - Family-Friendly with Parks
1. In the Lifestyle tab, scroll to the top
2. Check BOTH "Family-Friendly" AND "Walkable" checkboxes
3. Click "Find Neighborhoods That Match"
4. Results should show:
   - Both transit AND parks scores
   - "Great parks nearby" in reasons for some
   - Family-friendly indicators
