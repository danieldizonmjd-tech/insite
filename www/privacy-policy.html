Privacy Policy for InSite

Last Updated: November 18, 2025

1. Introduction

InSite ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide

  • Search queries (ZIP codes, cities, neighborhoods)
  • Comparison preferences (ZIP codes selected for comparison)

2.2 Automatically Collected Data

  • Device information (type, operating system version)
  • Usage statistics (features accessed, time spent)
  • Error logs and crash reports

2.3 Data We DO NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
  • Location data (GPS coordinates)
  • Financial information
  • Contacts or calendar data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide property market analysis and insights
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Debug and fix technical issues
  • Generate anonymous usage analytics

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit personal information to external servers except for:

  • Loading public property data from our GeoJSON database
  • Anonymous crash reports for app improvement

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all network requests
  • Input sanitization to prevent injection attacks
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) headers

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Chart.js (CDN): For data visualization (no data collection)
  • Google Fonts: For typography (no data collection)

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Data Retention

Search history and preferences are stored locally on your device and can be cleared by:

  • Clearing the app cache in Android settings
  • Uninstalling and reinstalling the app

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data stored by the app (via app settings)
  • Delete your data (by clearing app cache)
  • Opt-out of analytics (via app settings)

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@insiteatl.com
Website: https://insiteatl.com

11. Compliance

This app complies with:

  • Google Play Store Developer Program Policies
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable

12. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Internet Access: Required to load property data
  • Network State: To detect offline mode

We do NOT request permissions for:

  • Camera, Microphone, or Location
  • Contacts, Calendar, or SMS
  • Storage access (beyond app cache)
